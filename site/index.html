How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your music app

    Attune connects to the TIDAL or Spotify desktop app you already use. YouTube Music and Apple Music open in a browser window of their own.

  2. 2

    Start a session

    You get a four-letter code. Send it to your friends, or send a link that opens Attune and joins straight away.

  3. 3

    Hear the same thing

    Play, pause, seeking, skips and queue changes are mirrored to everyone, on whichever service they're using.

Installing

  1. Download and run the installer. It installs for your account only.
  2. Windows may say Windows protected your PC, because I'm just a dev and I don't have a certificate. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open your music app from Attune's connect screen, pick a name, and start or join a session.

Attune works with each service's own player, so you need whatever that service needs to play full songs. Without an active subscription, you will get ad breaks or short song previews.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your music app

    Attune connects to the TIDAL or Spotify desktop app you already use. YouTube Music and Apple Music open in a browser window of their own.

  2. 2

    Start a session

    You get a four-letter code. Send it to your friends, or send a link that opens Attune and joins straight away.

  3. 3

    Hear the same thing

    Play, pause, seeking, skips and queue changes are mirrored to everyone, on whichever service they're using.

Songs a service doesn't have

Each app looks every song up for itself. If someone's service is missing one, everyone sees who, and a song nobody can play is skipped.

No accounts

A code is all it takes to join. Attune doesn't sign you up for anything, and the server keeps sessions in memory only.

Out of the way

A small window that can shrink to a strip or stay in the tray, in your music app's colours if you like.

Updates when you say so

Attune tells you when a new version is out and installs it only when you choose to.

Installing

  1. Download and run the installer. It installs for your account only, no admin rights needed.
  2. Windows may say Windows protected your PC, because Attune is new and isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open your music app from Attune's connect screen, pick a name, and start or join a session.

Attune works with each service's own player, so you need whatever that service needs to play full songs. Without a subscription, YouTube Music plays ads; Attune steps out during one and rejoins afterwards.
